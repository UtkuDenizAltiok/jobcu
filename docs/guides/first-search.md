# Your first search

Open Jobcu with the launcher. Complete these steps inside the app; do not send keys or documents
in chat. The default **72-hour** window means ads posted in the previous three days, not a
three-day search runtime. Existing saved window choices are preserved.

## 1. Complete setup

The Search page's **Make Jobcu yours** list has three essentials:

1. **Set up AI:** choose your provider/key/model in Settings, save and test the connection.
   See [Set up your keys](getting-your-keys.md). Optional job-source keys are separate.
2. **Add CV** and **Add Cover letter:** upload PDF, Word or text documents.
3. Read **What Jobcu understood**. Add optional background in **Anything your documents don't
   say**, such as a newer language level or information needed for permit requirements.

Search becomes available when AI configuration and both documents are ready.

## 2. Run the search

1. In **Where do you want to work?**, write your places and conditions, such as *Dublin or Cork*
   or *Germany, within 50 minutes of a city centre by public transport*. Travel goes to the
   nearest edge of a place unless you ask for its centre; estimates are labelled.
2. Choose **Posted within: 72 hours** for the planned first review, and check the **job types**.
3. Click **Find matching jobs**. Keep the terminal open; watch progress while it runs.
   Duration depends on sources, countries, model and account limits.
4. Answer limit prompts. **Always** changes that search limit to **No limit** for future searches;
   monthly limits still apply and you can restore a number in Settings.

Your query decides countries and roles. Development priorities are not added to it automatically.

## 3. Read the results

| What you see | Meaning or action |
|---|---|
| **Score 0–100**, breakdown and reasons | Estimated fit, not hiring probability. **Limited to …** explains a blocker. Low-score jobs remain visible. |
| **Understood as → Edit** | Correct a condition and apply it to the jobs already found. Estimates and **not checked** conditions need your judgement. |
| Closing date, first-seen date or unknown date | Check freshness on the original ad. Expired jobs and old reposts are filtered when evidence establishes them. |
| Short-summary or excerpt warning | Open the original; missing requirements may change the fit. Online research does not guarantee a saved full ad. |
| Town from ad text/online, or unclear place | Review the location evidence before relying on it. Unknown towns remain in the list. |
| **Open job**, **Save**, **Applied**, **Not interested** | Open the original or record your choice. **Show hidden** lets you undo a dismissal. |
| **Search details** | Source availability, filtering, limits and time per step, including time waiting for answers. |
| **Left out by your conditions** | Inspect what your location conditions excluded. |

## 4. Review and control usage

**Score check** keeps a small sample for independent ratings and reveals scores after you rate.
Summary samples are labelled; read their originals first. Ratings remain private.

**Settings → Usage and limits** shows tokens and web requests. Add prices for an estimate and
set monthly/search limits; the provider's usage page is the billing reference.

After the planned first search finishes, use [Review a search](../PROMPTS.md#review-a-search)
in the local Jobcu project chat. Help: [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
